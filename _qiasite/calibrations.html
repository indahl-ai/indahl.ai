Echo on the control qubit during idles on a coupled pair
Spin echo for static coupling is standard practice		D1/R5Coherence an idling qubit loses over 30 us beside its coupled partner, as compiled / with the echo (fez 13-14, 17 Nov 2026): 59 % / 46 %. Where the coupling is weak (fez 39-53) the echo changes little: 43 % / 41 % The echo recovers the coupling share of the loss; the rest is ordinary dephasing, which this correction does not address.17 Nov 2026, fez, repeated run: an echo pulse on the control qubit returned about 13 percentage points of coherence on edge 13-14 (loss 59 % [56, 63] as compiled, 46 % [42, 50] with the echo) and left edge 39-53 about unchanged (43 % [39, 46], 41 % [37, 44]). On an uncoupled control pair the echo neither added nor cost coherence (0.977 [0.934, 1.019]), so the gain is the coupling removed. The measured coupling on those edges is 0.76x and 0.34x of the published value.
