Who it’s for
A quant desk at a bank or insurer that prices options and risk with quantum amplitude estimation on IBM hardware. They pay for these shots today.
Their run
The standard amplitude-estimation ladder, fitted with no allowance for gate error, on the layout IBM’s published record picks.
What we changed
The ladder loses signal to gate error, and the fit needs the real size of that error. Before each run we measured the error of the two gates the circuit uses, on the qubits it uses. In run 1 it came in at 1.45× to 1.49× IBM’s stated figure on ibm_kingston, 1.18× to 1.24× on ibm_marrakesh, and 0.69× to 0.72× on ibm_fez. The fit uses the measured error, with one scale per chip fixed from that chip’s earlier run and not fitted to these shots.
Outcome
Ours against the client: BELOW (lower error) on all three chips, in both runs. Ours against a correction from IBM’s stated figure: BELOW on ibm_kingston in both runs.
