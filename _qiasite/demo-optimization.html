Who it’s for
A logistics or scheduling team that runs QAOA the way IBM’s tutorial teaches, and reports the measured cut against the noiseless cut.
Their run
A max-cut problem on the chip’s own coupler map over its first 100 qubits (111 edges), one QAOA layer, IBM’s default layout, counts read raw.
What changed
The measured cut shrinks with gate error, and how much depends on the error of each coupler. IBM states that error per coupler. Before the run it was measured on the client’s own layout, with the gates running in the same layers as the client’s circuit. The cut is rescaled by the shrink that measured error predicts. Readout is not corrected in any arm.
Outcome
Calibrated against the client: ABOVE (higher ratio) on all three chips, in both runs. Calibrated against IBM’s stated figure: ABOVE on all three chips, in both runs.
