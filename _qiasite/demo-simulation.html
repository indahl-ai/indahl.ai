Who it’s for
A materials or energy company’s simulation team running time-stepped spin dynamics on IBM hardware and reading neighbor correlations from the Estimator. They compare late-time values against a classical reference.
Their run
A 10-qubit chain, 20 time steps, the layout IBM’s record picks, values read raw.
What changed
Gate error makes every value decay toward zero as the steps go on. Each value is corrected for that decay, using the gate error measured on the chain that day: 2.13 times IBM’s stated figure on ibm_kingston, 1.55 times on ibm_fez and 3.24 times on ibm_marrakesh in run 1. One exponent per chip was fixed from that chip’s earlier job and not fitted to these shots.
Outcome
Calibrated against the client: BELOW (closer to exact) on all three chips, in both runs.
