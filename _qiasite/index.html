What we found

  • A short echo during each readout cut the entanglement a readout costs neighboring qubit pairs from 0.25 to 0.010 of pair fidelity on ibm_kingston (17–18 Nov 2026).
  • Gate error measured above IBM’s stated figure in all 16 runs on 7 processors (23–24 Oct 2026); on 17 Nov the five kingston qubits measured sat around the published values.
  • Coupling on ibm_phoenix measured at 0.49 of the stated value (22 Oct 2026).
  • 4,204 of 4,204 coherence entries kept their value and date under newer record timestamps (16 Sep to 23 Oct 2026).
  • Each mid-circuit readout turns the phase of idle qubits by 1.75 to 2.51 degrees; the published properties carry no field for it (read 17 Nov 2026).

Services in detail

Method

Four outcomes, never folded

Every reading lands in one of four outcomes against what was stated.

ABOVE

The measured value sits above the stated value, outside the interval.

BELOW

The measured value sits below the stated value, outside the interval.

CONSISTENT

The stated value sits inside the interval. The bound is printed.

CANNOT TELL

The data cannot separate the stated value from the size that would matter.
