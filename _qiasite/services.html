Before buying

Common questions

What is delivered, and when?
A line-item report with an evidence package: stated, measured, interval and outcome, with the raw job records, run IDs, code and checksums. A 30-day pilot delivers signed receipts for each run, one variance report and the measured corrections. The delivery date is fixed in the statement of work before any data is taken.
What comes before any work?
A mutual NDA, then a scoping call, then a signed statement of work or order form. Nothing runs before it is signed.
How is payment made?
By invoice, carrying the case number, paid by bank transfer. Bank details arrive on a separate sheet, never in the body of an email. Prices are the ones listed on this page, and fees are independent of findings.
How long does an agreement run?
Every agreement runs for one year and does not renew. Only Heuremen can end it early, and if it does, prepaid time not yet used is refunded pro rata.
What data is handled?
Processor calibration records and job records, or a paper and its released data, not personal data. Hardware access stays on the client’s own account, through an instance with an allocation limit. API keys are never sent by email or chat.
Can the results be used to train an AI model?
No. Neither the report’s measurement tables nor any data supplied may be used to train, fine-tune or build AI models. Retrieval and quoting with attribution, to answer a question, are permitted.
Who owns the report?
The client, on full payment, along with the data from its own runs. Heuremen keeps its methods, code and measurement tables, licensed to the client for internal use with the report.
Is anything published?
Nothing about an engagement is published without the client’s written consent.
How an engagement runs

From scope to delivery

Fixed steps, the same for every client.

  1. Scope Mutual NDA, then the figures in question and the metrics of completion, set in writing.
  2. Measure Runs and recounts against the calibration record each job ran under.
  3. Report A line-item report: stated, measured, interval, outcome.
  4. Hand over The evidence package, rerunnable by the client or anyone the client names.
