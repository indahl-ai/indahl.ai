Stated update cadence against delivered

Government data portals publish, for each dataset, how often it is updated and when it last changed, in the standard data.json. This page holds the stated cadence against the last modified date. A series is on schedule if its margin, twice its stated interval minus its age, is above zero and behind if the margin is below zero. Read against that bound of zero a series is above or below it and never consistent (plan addendum of 12 November 2026). The interval used is at least two days, so a daily series is behind after four days, and a date with no time of day is read as the end of that day. Records that share a landing page are one series, because archived releases cannot change. The rules were committed before any portal was read, except that the series rule was decided after the first portal read and before any outcome was shown (plan, addendum 1).

Loading.

The modified date is what the publisher reports. On a sample of each portal’s download links, the file’s own date is compared with it and shown. Series with a cadence that is not a regular interval, or with no readable date, are counted as cannot tell (NO_INTERVAL_FORMABLE), since no margin can be formed. A portal whose file could not be read is listed apart and is in no share. A stated duration may carry a decimal, so R/P3.5D is 3.5 days, and when records of one series share the newest date and state different cadences, the series is read against the longest of them. Both rules were written on 10 November 2026, after the data, and the count under the old rules is shown beside the total (plan addendum 2). No ranking of agencies.

Plan, code and trend history for this page are public: cadence. Every plan was committed before the data it describes.
