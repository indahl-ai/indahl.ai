When a vendor says something ends on a date, does the date hold?

Model and API providers publish tables of what they are retiring and when. This page reads those tables from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Cloudflare on a schedule and keeps every reading. It lists what ends in the next 60 days, how much notice the tables that state a deprecation date gave, and every date that moved, was added or disappeared since the first reading. Open weight models carry no vendor shutdown date and are not in this version.

The plan was committed before the first reading. Each later change to it is a dated addendum in the plan: a parsing error found before anything was shown, a check of the saved pages before every pass, and a fix on 30 Oct for month names the reader had skipped. The test counts only dates that pass after watching started, so it starts empty and fills as dates pass. Every move is read in full on the vendor’s page. A moved date is not called a fault.

Held: a stated date passed and never changed while it was watched. Moved: it changed at least once before it passed. Removed early: the row left the page before its date. Fewer than 30 dates in the test reads cannot tell with the reason TOO_WIDE_TO_RESOLVE. Above is a lower end of the 95 percent interval at or above 5 percent, consistent is a whole interval below 5 percent, and 10 percent is the size that matters (plan addendum of 12 November 2026, Heuremen Standard). REQ-13 beside the test row gives the readings that returned nothing and the share of cannot tell by reason. Notice is the days from a stated deprecation date to the shutdown date. Lifespan is the days from release to shutdown and is not notice. Google names that contain preview are shown apart from the other names. Sources are the vendors’ own deprecation pages, linked in the code folder.
