Does the size in a model’s name match the weights?

Many model names state a size, such as 8B or 135M. For the most downloaded models on Hugging Face this page compares that size with the parameter count Hugging Face computes from the weight files. Names with no size, with several sizes, or with a mixture-of-experts pattern, and repositories that are quantized or packed, are not comparable and are counted apart.

The plan, with its dated addenda and notes, was committed before the first model was read. The band that counts as agreement was fixed before the first read. A row outside it is read in full and the reason written beside it. Rows not yet read in full are counted above the tables as not read in full. The page does not say that the name is wrong. Most rows are a name that states one part of a model or the model a draft checkpoint pairs with.

The outcome is read under the Heuremen Standard from 12 November 2026, with the rule in the plan addendum dated that day. The share of disagreements is read against zero. It reads ABOVE when its whole 95 percent interval is at or above 0.05, CONSISTENT when the whole interval is below 0.05, and otherwise CANNOT TELL with a reason. An interval that holds both 0 and 0.10 reads CANNOT TELL (TOO_WIDE_TO_RESOLVE). Fewer than 30 comparable rows also read CANNOT TELL (TOO_WIDE_TO_RESOLVE). Beside each share the page prints the count of failed reads and the share of CANNOT TELL split by reason.

The headline is read under the Heuremen Standard from 12 November 2026. The size that matters was named after these data were first read, so its outcome is defined after the data.

Agree: the weights hold between 0.70 and 1.35 times the stated size. Disagree: outside that band. Above: the lower end of the 95 percent interval of the disagreement share is above 0.10. Consistent: the upper end is below 0.05. Otherwise, and with fewer than 30 comparable models, cannot tell. The list of models where Hugging Face’s own total contradicts its own breakdown was defined after the first read and carries no outcome. Source: the Hugging Face API.
