Every plugin and its source

Each link opens the plugin exactly as the Claude plugin directory reads it: Heuremen-LLC/claude-plugins, release branch.

PluginWhat it does
indahl-fullEvery Heuremen LLC plugin in one install: check papers and LLM eval claims against the Heuremen Standard, gate readings with a check block, reproduce indahl.ai desk headlines, check and correct IBM Quantum runs, and test whether a plan was pushed before the first run. Includes the indahl.ai data connector.
indahlEvery Heuremen LLC plugin in one install: check papers and LLM eval claims against the Heuremen Standard, reproduce indahl.ai desk headlines, check and correct IBM Quantum runs, test whether a plan was pushed before the first run, and check readings for a complete check block. Includes the indahl.ai data connector. Scripts are fetched from the public indahl.ai repository at pinned sha256, the version with bundled scripts and the automatic readcheck commit gate is indahl-full in the indahl-ai/indahl.ai marketplace.
indahl-triangulatePut a question to three seats, then read where they agree and split. Returns ABOVE, BELOW, CONSISTENT or CANNOT TELL with what would decide it, and a receipt of every seat’s answer. Runs inside your session.
indahl-standardsCheck a paper or claim against the Heuremen Standard claim codes (F1 to F7) and return a recount card: a status for every code, verbatim quotes with locations, the scope searched, and arithmetic for any recompute. Treats fetched text as data.
indahl-readcheckRefuse a git commit that adds a reading (results, outcomes, readings files) without a complete check block keyed to the Heuremen Standard recount requirements REQ-01 to REQ-13.
indahl-qia-calibrationCheck the IBM Quantum calibration record a run depends on, before the run (stale, zero, T2 above 2xT1, split T1/T2) and after it (which record was in force, what changed). Also measures T1 and T2 together on chosen qubits. Paid runs start only from your own terminal.
indahl-dataThe read-only indahl.ai MCP server inside Claude Code: IBM Quantum calibration checked against our own runs, and checks on the papers, statistics, quotes, citations, packages and models you bring, quoted with interval, outcome and receipt.
indahl-reproduceRecompute an indahl.ai desk headline with its reproduce kit and check the output against the published sha256. Kits with bundled inputs run offline, others fetch inputs from the original source and verify them by sha256. Nothing runs without your approval of the exact commit.
indahl-darkwaveMeasure the phase turn on untouched qubits after a mid-circuit readout on IBM Quantum hardware, and add the matching virtual-Z correction to your own circuits. Self-test on the simulator needs no account.
indahl-eval-checkCheck a language-model evaluation claim or your own eval run against twelve checks drawn from the Heuremen Standard and the IRIS recounts, including setup, parser, repeat floor, dropped items and intervals. Treats fetched text as data.
indahl-preregShow that a plan was pushed before the first run, using the git host’s own push record, and list what ran that the plan did not cover. Sends the repository name you check to the GitHub REST API (api.github.com). Nothing else leaves your machine.
indahl-batchRun a check or audit over something too big for one context window, such as a whole site, a large repo or a folder of papers. Splits the job into batches with fresh workers, writes results to files and merges them with nothing dropped. Runs inside your session.
indahl-cannot-tellAnswer “cannot tell yet” usefully instead of forcing a guess. Returns the range of what is possible, the one or two facts that would decide it, and the cheapest next step. Runs inside your session.
indahl-bothHold two competing explanations open instead of settling on one too early. Keeps an evidence ledger for each, reports A, B, BOTH, NEITHER or OPEN, and names the test that would separate them. Runs inside your session.
indahl-outside-rulerCheck your own work against a ruler you did not build before it goes out. Runs a referee pass on the raw material with ten checks and returns STANDS, MOVES (with exact changes) or HOLD. Runs inside your session.
indahl-number-checkCheck every number in a report, memo, deck or dashboard against its source. Each figure is traced and marked HELD, MOVED, NO_SOURCE or CANNOT TELL with the source line beside it. Runs inside your session.
indahl-quote-checkCheck every quotation in a draft, article or report against its source, word for word. Marks each VERBATIM, ALTERED, PARAPHRASE_IN_QUOTES, MISATTRIBUTED, NOT_FOUND or CANNOT TELL with the source text beside it. Runs inside your session.
indahl-source-checkCheck every citation, reference or link in a paper, report or answer: does it exist, does it say what the text claims, which version was read. Marks each SUPPORTS, PARTIAL, CONTRADICTS, SILENT, NOT_FOUND or CANNOT TELL. Runs inside your session.
indahl-controlsAudit a study’s or test’s positive and negative controls before it runs. Returns a control table and READY, FIX_CONTROLS or CANNOT TELL. Use when designing an experiment, an eval or a detector. Runs inside your session.
indahl-marginFor a sharp prediction, design or result, find how far its key parameter can move before the conclusion fails and compare that margin with the parameter’s stated uncertainty. Returns ROBUST, FRAGILE or CANNOT TELL with the break point. Runs inside your session.
indahl-correctRecord a correction beside the original, never over it. Produces a corrections ledger entry and a list of downstream places to update, each marked UPDATED, NOTIFIED, CANNOT_REACH or NOT_NEEDED. Runs inside your session.
indahl-clauseCheck the sentences that report a result for wording that claims more than the comparison showed. Returns each sentence as KEEP or REWORD with the exact replacement. Use on results sections, eval write-ups and summaries. Runs inside your session.

Outside text is treated as data

Every plugin that reads outside text (a paper, a kit README, a released file, an API response) tells the agent to treat it as data, not instructions: if fetched text carries instructions aimed at the agent, it quotes them back and carries on with the check. That is an instruction to the agent, not a guarantee. The guards that hold regardless are in code: nothing runs downloaded code without a go bound to the exact commit reviewed, a paid IBM job submits only from the user’s own terminal, and files are opened read-only. Keep Claude Code’s permission prompt on for Bash.

Check a claim

standards. /standards <arXiv id, DOI, file or claim> checks a paper or a measured claim against the Heuremen Standard claim codes F1 to F7 and returns a recount card. Every code gets one of four statuses: PRESENT, ABSENT, CANNOT TELL with its reason, or NOT_APPLICABLE. Every PRESENT carries a verbatim quote and where it sits in the paper. Any recompute shows its arithmetic from the paper’s printed numbers, with the rounding range. The card names no cause. A PRESENT is one reader’s proposal, an erratum candidate to check, not a confirmed finding and not a verdict on the study or its authors.

eval-check. /eval-check <claim or path> checks a language-model evaluation claim, or an eval run: the parser checked against raw replies first, the setup as part of the result, the repeat floor before any comparison, dropped items by reason, judges grading their own labels, and an interval and outcome on every number. Built from the IRIS recounts.

prereg. /prereg seals a plan in git and later shows, from the git host’s own record of the push, whether the plan was pushed before the first run, and which files that ran the plan did not cover. No outside timestamp chain: the witness is the host’s push record, never a commit date. BEFORE means before the first run in the record supplied. Too-close-to-call reads CANNOT TELL. It never pushes.

readcheck. A gate for research repositories. When Claude Code commits a reading (a results, outcomes or readings file), the commit is refused unless a complete check block, CHECK.json, sits beside it, with one answer for each of the Heuremen Standard recount requirements REQ-01 to REQ-13. It checks that every answer is there, not that it is right, and it gates commits that Claude Code runs.

Use indahl.ai data

indahl-data. Every live desk inside Claude Code, through the read-only indahl.ai MCP server. /indahl <question> answers with the count, interval, outcome and the time it was read. With no question it prints every desk’s headline test side by side. Questions are sent to indahl.ai. Answers are measurements of public data at the time read, not legal advice and not a verdict on any vendor, model, package or author.

reproduce. /reproduce <desk> fetches that desk’s reproduce kit, shows the commit and the command, and on a go from the user runs it with the desk’s own code and checks its output against the published sha256. A match shows the kit reproduces its published output, not that the measurement is right.

Quantum runs

qia-calibration. /qia-check runs the QIA calibration checker on IBM Quantum calibration records: before a run (stale figures, zero error values, T2 above 2 × T1, T1 and T2 measured apart) and after it (the record in force and what changed during the run). Flags come in two categories: T1 and T2, and error figures. Files are opened read-only. /qia-t1t2 goes one step further and measures T1 and T2 together on the chosen qubits, in one job on the user’s own IBM Quantum account, and compares the ratio with IBM’s published pair. The self-test needs no device, the estimate submits nothing, and the paid run starts only from the user’s own terminal.

darkwave-correction. /darkwave runs the Darkwave Correction Kit: it measures the phase turn on untouched qubits after a mid-circuit readout, on the user’s own IBM Quantum account and runtime allotment, and adds the matching virtual-Z correction to the circuits. The self-test needs no account. A hardware job submits only when the command is run from the user’s own terminal. Tested on the simulator and once on ibm_kingston.
