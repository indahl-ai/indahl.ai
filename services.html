Services

Published claims checked against the data behind them.

Engagements

QIA · Quantum Idle Audit. Hardware runs and recounts on quantum processors, checked against the vendor’s published calibration and against the claims in a study. Each line carries the processor, the date, the calibration record it ran under and an interval.

IRIS · Inorganic Research & Intelligence Systems. Model evaluations and AI-assisted research recounted from released outputs, with the setup named on both sides.

Custom small models: compact open-weight models fine-tuned only on the client’s data for a defined task, delivered with their evaluation on the client’s own held-out data.

SAGAR · Standards Audit of Gravitational and Astrophysical Research. A gravitational-wave or cosmology paper read in full, with each place it disagrees with itself quoted, located and recomputed where the data allows.

What is delivered

  • A line-item report. Each line gives the figure checked, the stated value, the measured or recounted value with its interval, and one of four outcomes: ABOVE, BELOW, CONSISTENT or CANNOT TELL. Example: QIA-2026-001, an audit of IBM Heron calibration records, opened 22 October 2026 and issued 23 October 2026: report (PDF) · data and code (DOI).
  • Metrics of completion, established in advance.
  • An evidence package: raw job records and run IDs, code and checksums, so any line can be rerun independently.

Confidentiality

  • A commercial relationship connected to an entry on a public list is flagged on that entry, as set out under Conflict of interest.
