When a detector maker states a false positive rate, what do independent tests show?

Makers of tools that flag machine-written text state how often their tool marks human writing as machine-written. That rate matters most, since a false positive accuses a person who wrote their own words. This desk sets each stated rate beside independent tests that release their data, text by text, and reads the measured rate on known-human text against the stated one. It is not a ranking of detectors, and nothing on this page orders them.

The plan, with the detectors, the tests, the outcome rule and the headline cell, was fixed and committed before any test result was read (indahl-ai/calibrations, commit c9e4f8a, 22 November 2026). Every stated figure on this page was found word for word in the page as this run saved it, and the outcome rule passed its planted cases.

The independent tests

RAID (Dugan and others, ACL 2024, paper, data, MIT license). The released data hold the texts and their labels and no detector output. Detector scores are released only in the RAID leaderboard’s predictions on its test split, whose labels are kept hidden, so no score can be set beside a known-human text. The GPTZero leaderboard entry is dated 7 October 2025 and the Winston AI entry 24 September 2026. No Originality.ai entry is in the leaderboard. RAID’s own false positive figure is fixed at 5 percent by design and is never read here as a measured rate.

Liang and others 2023, “GPT detectors are biased against non-native English writers” (paper, data, MIT badge on the repository). Detectors were queried on 15 March 2023. The human text read here is the four folders the repository labels human-written whose words no model edited:

  • Real CS224N: 145 texts, Stanford CS224N course project abstracts, mean 179.6 words
  • Real College Essays: 70 texts, US college admission essays, mean 611.5 words
  • US 8th grade essay: 88 texts, US 7th to 10th grade student essays (Hewlett), mean 378.0 words
  • Real TOEFL: 91 texts, TOEFL essays by non-native English writers, mean 107.1 words

The folders of human essays rewritten or polished by a model are left out, since a model changed their words. For each text the release keeps a score from each detector and no label.

Jabarian and Imas 2025, NBER working paper 34223 (paper, data, MIT license). The release holds 2,100 rows of human text, 1,992 distinct passages once 108 repeated rows are counted once (5 repeats carry other verdicts, and the first is kept). Passages run from 1852 to 2019, a mean of 693.2 words, across six genres: amazon review 200, blog 200, news 300, novel 1,000, restaurant review 100, resume 192. Every passage has its text, and none is a placeholder. The release keeps the classification Originality.ai returned for each passage, the prediction label Pangram returned, and a GPTZero score field. The paper names the API versions (Pangram v2025-05, GPTZero v2, Originality.ai Turbo) and no date on which the detectors were queried.

Reading, delivered relative to stated: above if the whole interval is over the stated rate or ceiling, below if the whole interval is under a stated point, consistent if the interval holds the stated point or lies at or under the stated ceiling, otherwise cannot tell. A stated figure is read only when it was in force on the date the test queried the detector, read from the Internet Archive’s capture of the maker’s own page on or before that date. Cannot tell carries one reason. DATE_UNMATCHED means the test names no query date, or no capture of the page exists on or before it. NO_OPERATING_POINT means the test kept only a score and the maker states no numeric cut-off, and this desk never picks one. VERSION_UNMATCHED means a rate outside the figure where the versions differ or one is not named. STRADDLES EDGE means the interval crosses the figure. Where a rate was measured, the column beside the reading says where it lies against the figure on the maker’s page today. That column decides nothing. Beside the pairings: 3 NOTHING RETURNED. Accuracy, the secondary reading

A stated accuracy is read only where the test’s mix of human and machine text matches the mix the maker’s figure describes. No test in the plan matches a stated mix, so every accuracy pairing with released output reads cannot tell (MIX_DIFFERS): 0 above, 0 below, 0 consistent, 5 cannot tell. 3 more returned no per-item output.

What this page holds and what it leaves out

Every reading prints the test’s date and the detector version it ran. Detectors change often, so a test from 2023 or 2025 describes that version and no later one. A reading says how a stated rate and a measured rate compare. It does not say why they differ. ZeroGPT appears in the tests and states no figure this plan read, so it is left out.
